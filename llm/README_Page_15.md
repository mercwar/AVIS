## AVIS and Collaboration Workflows
AVIS enhances collaboration by embedding **shared context** directly into artifacts. Teams can coordinate across projects and environments without ambiguity, since every file carries its own metadata.

---

### Collaboration Benefits
- **[Shared context](ca://s?q=Shared_context_in_AVIS)** — headers ensure all contributors understand the artifact’s purpose.  
- **[Team coordination](ca://s?q=Team_coordination_with_AVIS)** — versioning and author fields track contributions.  
- **[Cross-environment consistency](ca://s?q=Cross_environment_consistency_in_AVIS)** — AIFVS mappings standardize classification across teams.
