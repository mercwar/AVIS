AVIS and Disaster Recovery

AVIS strengthens disaster recovery by embedding rollback, recovery, and continuity metadata directly into artifacts. This ensures that systems can recover quickly and maintain operational integrity during failures.

Disaster Recovery Benefits

  • Rollback metadata — headers define recovery points for artifacts.
  • Continuity planning — metadata supports business continuity strategies.
  • Error isolation — corrupted files are quarantined automatically.
  • Resilience — artifacts include recovery instructions for rapid restoration.

Example AVIS Header (Recovery Config)

```yaml

AVIS-ARTIFACT

Filename: recoveryPlan.yml

AIFVS-MAP: RecoveryArtifact

Description: Disaster recovery plan for FireGem engine deployment

Author: Demon

Created: 2026-08-03

Version: 1.0.0

recovery:

  • id: rollbackStage action: “Revert to last stable build”
  • id: restoreData action: “Reload from backup dataset”
  • id: validateIntegrity action: “Run AVIS validation loop”
